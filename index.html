04 / The kind, fair-play bits

Good people.
Good building.

A few simple guidelines keep the weekend welcoming, fair, and fun for everyone. More event-specific details will be shared with registered teams.

✓
Who can join

Current students of the college, from any department or year. Bring a team of 2–4 students; solo registrations can ask organizers for a match.

✓
Build during the event

Start a new project at kickoff. You may use existing libraries, APIs, and design tools; disclose them in your final submission.

✓
Be thoughtful with data

Use mock or public data only. Don't collect real personal, health, or sensitive information from students.

✓
Make it welcoming

Treat participants, mentors, and volunteers with respect. Harassment or discriminatory behavior is not okay.

✓
Submit and demo

Submit a working prototype, a short project description, and a 3-minute demo by the announced deadline.

✓
How judging works

Proposed criteria: usefulness (30%), working prototype (30%), creativity (20%), and clarity of demo (20%). Judges’ decisions are final.
